ProfileVanessa Woenam Banini · The founder issue · No. 01p. 02

Shea kernels gathered in the Northern Region

The thesis, in one line

My mother never called it skincare. She rubbed shea butter into our skin every morning because that was simply what we knew. Today I run Banini Butter, a whipped shea brand made in Accra from nuts gathered by women's cooperatives near Tamale.

The gap was never the butter. It was everything around it: the texture, the packaging, the standard. My work is giving ingredients Ghana already has the brand they deserve, so they reach the highest shelves without pretending to be from anywhere else.

Work

What I've built, newest first.

Now
  1. 2026 · Founder

    Legalite

    My company, building a legaltech app. I'm pitching it at the Accra LawTech Hangout on 3 December.

  2. 2026 · Founder

    Banini Butter

    Whipped shea butter in four blends, sourced from women's cooperatives near Tamale and made in small batches in Accra.

  3. 2026 · Winner

    TV3 The Top Intern, Season 3

    Won the season on national television, with a full Master's scholarship from Academic City University.

  4. 2025 · Chairperson

    Educational and Research Committee, ASSAG

    Chaired the committee at the Actuarial Science Students' Association of Ghana for a little over a year. Held the Beyond Insurance seminar to show students the range of careers actuarial science opens up. At the end of my tenure I was voted Student Activist of the Year and Best Committee Chairperson.

  5. 2024 · 2nd runner-up

    Tetrol, MTN Business Challenge

    Pitched Tetrol, a self-heating, self-cooling lunchbox and bottle flask I designed, at the UPSA Business Challenge 2024, sponsored by MTN Pulse, and placed second runner-up.
