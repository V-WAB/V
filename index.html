Profilep. 02

To you who found this page,

Most of what you will see here began before I felt ready. I did not wait for permission, and neither should you.

I trained to measure risk, and the lesson I keep returning to is simple: the greater risk is waiting. Waiting to be chosen, to be certain, to feel qualified. Start where you are, with what you have, and let the work teach you the rest.

Whatever you are carrying, an idea, a skill, a question no one has answered yet, it is worth building. Build it well, build it here, and as you rise, keep the door open for whoever comes next.

With love and blessings,Vanessa (Woenam) Banini
